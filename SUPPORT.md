# Support

For installation questions and reproducible bugs, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/KSAGlory/KSA-Police/issues). Include your MTA and Slothbot versions, relevant log messages, and the steps to reproduce. Remove passwords, IP addresses, account records, and tokens from logs first.

This is a community foundation, not a turnkey replacement for single-player police AI. Help with new pursuit features is best proposed as a focused issue or pull request.
